Draymus Mortiar, LE - Necromancer, Dhampir
El Guapo, NE - Swashbuckler, Human, Pirate (one session only)

Normally this adventure path starts in Longacre, but we’re going to have to get there first.  We begin in the city of Kintargo.

Recent events: A rebellion has begun in Cheliax. Martial Law has been declared in most major cities. The Lord-Mayor of Kintargo has disappeared and shortly after, Barzillai Thrune, Inquisitor of Asmodeus and cousin to the Queen has appeared to take command.  Three days later, a young Samurai named Freya receives orders to participate in a massive covert operation within the city.

The Samurai is of a lesser noble house, and has trained all her life to serve her country and Queen.  Her co-lead on this mission is a cleric of Asmodeus, the official religion of Cheliax.  They assemble a team suited for dirty work and time their strike to take place concurrently with many other similar operations on what will be called the Night of Ashes.

Their orders are specifically to confirm a report that there is a secret temple to a disallowed religion underneath a musical instrument shop called the Silver Star.  This shop is owned by the famous actress Shensen but she is not home when the team arrives.

Their scouts make quick work of the shopkeeper and their Asmodean Priest instantly finds the secret temple entrance. After a long protracted battle, the secret temple to Saranrae is destroyed along with all it’s inhabitants.  Among the slain is Viggo, the Dawnflower Swordmaster.  His prized scimitar is taken and replaced with a rapier.

The temple is spiritually and magically dismantled. The hobgoblin is carried out on a stretcher.  False evidence is planted and the building burns, collapsing part of tunnels leading to the former temple.

Freya arrives on the scene with Dottari Guards just in time to fail to fight the fire and declare the incident an unfortunate accident.  She collects payment from her contractor whom she knows only as “Nox.”  Nox appears to be a female human with some manner of power or possession about her. She wields a fine looking Glaive and pays the team 100gp each before sending them to a safe house.  From the balcony they watch three separate fires blaze out of control across the city.

After laying low at the lavishly appointed safehouse for nearly 24 hours, the team is transported in covered wagon to the docks, where they step aboard the “Western Star,” a pleasure cruiser bound for Remesiana, in the middle-South of Cheliax.  For a week they will relax and move steadily farther away from Kintargo and any investigation that could link them to the Night of Ashes.

Pleasure cruises never quite live up to expectations, or do they?  Aboard The Western Star, the team finds peace, relaxation, and even restoration as they sail to the nearest city West of Kintargo, a well known ocean port called Pezzack.

Here they fence their stolen goods (musical instruments, taken from the Silver Star music-shop they destroyed).  The team is sadly a bit light on people-skills and has no experience haggling so they are not able to secure top dollar, but they do well enough.

Worse than the price gouging is the local awareness of sea-going criminals such as one of the party members: El Guapo.  A known miscreant, El Guapo was formally charged by the Chelish Navy with Piracy, thievery, robbery, murder, assault, and disorderly conduct after his capture in a short but bloody sea battle near Kintargo around 2 months ago. 

In that time, as is custom with the Navy, these charges were shared with and brought against Mr. Guapo in all coastal cities of Cheliax.  Some are unaware he has been captured, as is the case in pezzack where his handsome visage can be seen on any of the dozens of bounty-posts all over the port quarter of the city.

El Guapo is recognized on his way back to the ship from vising a blacksmith downtown (Hired to alter the appearance of the Scimitar of Saranrae formerly owned by Viggo, Dawnflower instructor of Kintargo). 

The team spends the night aboard The Western Star and returns to town in the morning to conclude their business before departing.  It is then, when he is weaponless, that El Guapo is ambushed by a three man team of thugs, pirates without a ship in fact.  Luckily, Jamal has tailed him out of pure curiousity, and they make quick work of the brigands.

El Guapo is confined to the ship for the rest of the journey to avoid further troubles, but this does not ensure a safe voyage.  The Western Star sails through normally safe waters, but only a week before there was a large ship to ship combat between the Navy and the Reclamation (the rebellion).  The Navy won this exchange, but it was taxing enough to force all the surviving ships into port for days if not weeks, leaving the seas West of Cheliax exposed to opportunistic pirates.

And so the lookout of The Western Star spies a sail on the horizon, trailing the ship.  Mildred, their captain, offers her spyglass to El Guapo who estimates them to be a poor and desperate group of inexperienced pirates and the team decides to rid the seas of them.  They allow the pirates to think they are a defenseless and incompetent vessel, drawing them in closer and appearing to surrender until the pirates engage in a boarding maneuver.

In an intense and brutal battle, the enemy captain is killed and the pirates immediately surrender.  Operating both ships with a skeleton crew borrowed from Mildred or pressed into service at sword point, the team limps the two vessels into port at Corentyn, near the Arch of Aroden.

At port, they find greater success in trade and negotiation this time around.  They sell the pirate ship to the Chelaxian government, turn over their prisoners and bodies for bounty money (including planting the stolen Scimitar of Saranrae formerly owned by Viggo on the pirate captain’s corpse), and purchase superior armor and weapons with their newfound wealth.

Setting sail for their ultimate destination, Remesiana, they are emboldened, healed, and well outfitted for their next mission.  All they know is, their Samurai, Freya, will meet her contact Cimri Staelish to begin work for a person known only as Rezelago, apparently an important servant of the crown….

With their lovely pleasure cruise finally completed at Port Remesiana in lovely Southern Cheliax, the bad-guys disembark feeling rested, well fed, and generally pretty good about life.  They purchase some more goods. Armor for Jason, their Cleric of Asmodeus.  Wands and scrolls for Draymus and masterwork weapons for Jamal are acquired.  They enjoy a day of shopping before heading to meet Freya’s contact.

All they know about their new employer is that his name is Razelago, and he has connections with the ruling family of Cheliax, the Thrunes.  His emissary is a young woman named Cimri Staelish. She is somewhat immature and inexperienced, but talented and resourceful.  She rents horses for those that don’t own one (Freya of course prefers to ride Slepnir, her war horse) and they ride to Longacre, a rural town to the Northwest.

Their first “job” together is the robbery of the Louslik Tannery, one mile West of Longacre. After turning in their rented horses, they travel to the site and send in Jamal, their scout. The scout avoids detection as he climbs over the outer palisade wall of the tannery, and surveys the scene of lye pools (which come with a very powerful stench), and sees the office they will need to infiltrate.

As Jamal is sneaking closer to the target, a delivery of horse dung arrives at the main gates and the rogue opts for desperate measures. He immerses himself fully in one of the pools of shit, rotten eggs, and other chemicals.  As it happens, he has done so in the pit that is receiving the delivery, so he also suffers a wheelbarrow full of horse dung to be deposited on his person.


As the farmer leaves with his empty wheelbarrow, Jamal exits the lye pool and prepares to sap-ambush the owner’s dog which he fails spectacularly, alerting both the dog and consequently the owner to his presence.  As the owner exits his office, he sees what appears to be a gigantic goblin covered in shit attacking his dog with a club, so he fires his crossbow but misses.

The rest of the team comes to rescue and after a religious sermon and some brilliant diplomacy, they leave with the owners payroll box and no bloodshed.

Cleaning up their rogue at the river, they head to what Cimri calls “The Ash House,” her temporary home and Razelago’s meeting site.  The house is guarded by a Krenshar, a terrifying big-cat type predator.


The boss himself is also a frustrating mystery.  He appears merely as a silhouette against several thin paper screens.  Speaking in a raspy voice he thanks them for completion of Cimri’s mission and rewards them with a feast of food and wine as well as 100gp each.   
They are allowed to stay at the Ash House, a disappointing proposition after a week’s pleasure cruise, but they bed down. Hearing strange splashing noises upstairs, they eventually get curious and send Jamal to investigate the unexplored rooms of the house.  In the bathroom he finds a large porcelain tub filled with dirty water.  Unfortunately for Jamal, the water is an ectoplasmic undead lying in wait.

Jamal is grabbed and forced inside her ghostly form and he begins to drown. Before he is fully immersed, he manages to scream the team’s safe word “Kendrick Lamar!” and the team rushes to his rescue.  Draymus, using his skills as a necromancer is able to control and calm the undead and they learn that it was created when the owner of this house was burned alive inside it as she hid in her bathtub with her child and war medals.

Meeting with Razelago again the next morning, they learn that their next mission is to watch and disrupt a public sermon by the local priestess of Iomedae this very afternoon. Razelago believes she will be sowing the seeds of rebellion and that would interfere with his plans to unify the community.

Longacre is a unique town in Cheliax. Many retired soldiers make their home here, as well as many discontented former patriots.  The fact that the temple at it’s center is dedicated to worship of Iomedae, the Inheritor, is evidence enough that this place is a little ‘different.’

Merchants passing through speak of the rebellion, the so called “Glorious Reclamation,” as a distant idea but one that many would welcome here.  The Thrunes, the ruling family of Cheliax, are not well liked.  The Archbaron who represents the royal family in this county has a reputation for being somewhat removed from day to day business and citizenry.

And so the team begins to pull the strings tighter for Razelago, who asks them to view and counteract a sermon from the Sword Knight of Iomedae that oversees the Longacre Church.  The mysterious man fears this sermon may undermine his goals of unifying the town and suppressing it’s rebellious tendencies.  He presents them with red sashes embroidered with the Archbaron’s family crest, and the cross of Cheliax.

With Cimri Staelish, their contact and colleague in tow, they visit the church square and see that hundreds of townsfolk have already gathered.  The local police force is also in attendance, as the sheriff herself strides up to the group to speak with Cimri.

The team is surprised to learn that the sheriff and Cimri are aunt and niece to each other, and not on the best of terms. They have a brief, tense exchange that the team interrupts, much to Cimri’s relief.  They detect more than a little suspicion on Sheriff Rhona Staelish’s part towards their possibly involvement in the Louslik Tannery’s recent financial troubles.

The sermon, as they feared, attempts to sow the seeds of rebellion. It teases treason and exalts the great deeds of the glorious reclamation and claims that Iomedae herself has sent them one of her servants to help them reclaim their honor and bring justice to Longacre. A mysterious figure called the Angel Knight.  As proof of this divine assistance, the priestess’s twin sons bring out the beautifully crafted helm of the Angel Knight and display it in front of the church.

The crowd is inspired, impressed, and deeply curious.  The party goes to work immediately.  The Samurai Freya speaks out against this rebellious talk, openly defying the priestess and her sons.  Jamal eavesdrops on and interferes in several conversations, convincing the locals that the Archbaron does not approve of this sermon or any of Longacre’s people dwelling on such subject matter.    Eventually the crowd is dispersed with, at worst, thinly veiled threats of reprisal.

Returning to Razelago, they are thanked for their interference in the sermon and told they will have an even more eventful day tomorrow.  They rest at the Ash House (and advance to level 2).

Today, Razelago informs them, they will deliver the Archbaron’s decrees.  They are asked to do so as uneventfully as possible. This is unlikely, considering there is a separate degree that they are also commanded to deliver removes Rhona Staelish from the position of sheriff…  

The public decree is as follows:
"All Citizens of and Visitors to the Town of Longacre,

By Order of His Lordship Darellus Fex, Archbaron of Longacre, and in accordance with the wishes of Her Infernal Majestrix, Queen Abrogail II of the Thrice-Damned House of Thrune, the following decrees are now in effect:

Longacre's western gate, known as the Castle Gate, is closed until further notice. All travel into and out of Longacre must employ the north or south gates and be officially sanctioned. Only the archbaron's agents, sanctioned local businesses, or those displaying a writ of passage bearing the seal of House Fex, will be granted passage. All others must obtain a writ from the Longacre notary or do business elsewhere.

Longacre citizens shall not congregate in groups numbering greater than six in private, or in numbers greater than twelve in public. Exceptions will be made only for families with more than six members or for individuals undertaking the natural course of their business. Gatherings for the purposes of celebration, education, or religion are not exempt from this order.

A curfew is now in effect. From the hour after dusk to the hour before dawn, no citizen of Longacre is allowed upon the town's streets without a writ of exception bearing the seal of House Fex. The archbaron's agents and sanctioned local businesses are exempt from this order. Those seeking a writ of exception may apply at the Longacre Notary.

Any persons found in violation of these mandates or any other laws and sanctions of Longacre will be considered seditious agents and are subject to questioning, imprisonment, and summary judgment by the Archbaron of Longacre and officials of the town government.

For the prosperity of Longacre, the protection of the Empire, and the glory of House Thrune,

Darellus Fex,

Archbaron of Longacre"

They are commanded to place a copy of the decree at the town event post, the Last Stand Tavern, the Western Gate, and the town jail.  Most of these postings go smoothly thanks to stealth and/or diplomacy but they know they are in for trouble at the jail when they see the sheriff and four deputies are present.

Rhona takes the firing as calmly as possible, though she is clearly shocked and upset.  She leaves her Sheriff’s longsword and vest, takes her personal crossbow, and leaves without a word to the team or Cimri, her niece.

The deputies, after recovering from their initial disbelief, are very angry.  They also throw down their vests and storm out of the jail.  The team prepares to leave to report their mission accomplished, but they are ambushed by the four deputies who apparently have waited outside for them with saps.  After a brief and very one-sided battle, the former lawmen are locked in the jail and Cimri is left to watch the building while the party returns to inform Razelago.

The team is compensated for their work, and Razelago informs them that the Archbaron would like to see them at his residence, first thing in the morning.

The team is relaxing at the Ash house after firing Sheriff Rhona Staelish and being assaulted by four deputies loyal to her.  The night passes peacefully.

In the early morning they walk through Longacre to Scarlet Manor, the Baron’s home.  Along the way they notice that their decree still stands on the notice post where they left it, but the copy they posted on the beams of the Last Stand Tavern has been torn to shreds and left on the ground.  They make a mental note to thoughtfully craft a measured response to this act of defiance.

Halfing slave butlers and servants greet the team at the gates and offer to lead them to the Archbaron’s office.  While walking across Scarlet Manor’s grounds they see many armored guards with crossbows at the ready, both on foot and on the rooftops.  Ascending the beautiful black wood staircase, they are left at the door to Fex’s office.  They enter.

The office is beautiful. A recurring them of black wood and red drapes, carpet, and tapestries gives it a dark and ominous feel. Above the Archbaron’s gigantic blackwood desk are bloodied antlers locked in eternal contest, the symbol of House Fex.  The Archbaron Darellus Fex greets them as colleagues and offers them a great opportunity: to join him in bringing law and order to Longacre.

He asks them to swear the Hellfire Compact.   A legally binding but spiritually meaningless oath to the ruling family of Cheliax, House Thrune.  He himself has sworn this oath, and requires that they also take it if they are to become his official agents.  They are each required to recite the oath and place their hand on a black stone from the pits of hell. It burns everyone’s hand to touch it, except Jason sees this ritual very differently.

The Tiefling Cleric of Asmodeus fears no fire, least of all that fire from Hell where his ancestors were born.  He drops his pants, and backs his ass up to the hellstone as he swears his oath.  Maintaining intense eye contact with the team and with the Archbaron, he touches his bare ass cheek to the hellstone and completes the ritual.

Normally such bizarre behavior might offend the Archbaron and his sophisticated sensibilities, but Freya the Samurai taps into his scholarly curiosity, spinning Jason’s actions as “a very intimate part of his strange tiefling culture rarely seen by outsiders.”  The Archbaron is charmed by Freya’s interpretation and considers the ritual complete.

Archbaron Fex appoints them his official agents, instructs them to keep wearing their sashes at all times (some players are way ahead of the curve on this practice). 

The Archbaron has no set plan for how to locate and destroy rebellious factions within the town, but Razelago does. He demands that his servant reveal himself, saying there is no further need for theatrics where his trusted agents are concerned.  A familiar voice complies with the Archbaron, and a curtain is thrown back to reveal Razelago’s true form.

A bulbous fly body, a fat human infant upper torso and head, insect mandibles flexing out of the flabby mouth, and wings is not at all what the team expected. Their revulsion is obvious and universal and they ask the Archbaron to throw the curtain back over him please.  Razelago dismisses their reaction as typical prejudice against his kind, which is of course, an accuser devil of Hell.

Attempting to move past the awkward moment in the room, Razelago outlines some information the party already partially has.  Riley Kels, the bard of Longacre that frequently works in the Last Stand Tavern, was heard to be singing his angel songs again last night that detail his supposed meeting with the Angel Knight herself.  And the owner of the Last Stand Tavern, Bolgart, is known to have allowed illegal gatherings at his bar last night in excess of the Archbaron’s Decree’s limit of 5 non family persons in public.

Desperate to get out of the room with Razelago, the party takes their leave and heads to the tavern.  Positioning Jamal the Rogue at the backdoor, they re-apply the Archbaron’s decree to the front post of the Last Stand Tavern and hear an angry voice cursing them from inside.  Appearing at the door is Bolgart Caggan, owner/operator wielding a frying pan and metal spoon. As Freya attempts to calm the man, he attacks, hurling his frying pan at her and striking her in the helmet with it.  The party flies into action.

In just a few short moments, the fight is over. Bolgart is subdued. Riley Kels is captured by Jamal as he attempts to sneak out the backdoor.  The citizens in the bar are instructed to return to their homes and the barmaids that were about to join Bolgart in the melee are backed-down by Freya’s stern gaze and words of warning.

Bolgart is placed in the pillory stocks in front of the Longacre Jail and Riley is locked in a jail cell and interrogated thoroughly.  It seems he did indeed meet the Angel Knight one morning on a hill outside of town where he saw her praying and mistook her for an angel.

Later in this same day, they receive a letter from Razelago which was sent from the town Doctor, Gerya Rohalendi, to her cousin in Remesianna.  How Razelago came by this letter they do not know, but read it instantly.  

It seems the doctor’s hospice business is nearly 300gp in debt and may have to close soon.  Pretending to accidentally received and read the letter, they speak to the doctor and offer to have the Archbaron forgive her unpaid taxes because she is an asset to the community.  In so doing, they make an ally of the Doctor, a Cleric of Abadar.

With Riley Kels in tow at all times, they repeatedly discuss how much they would like to kill Riley Kels.

Following this, they get curious about the Angel Knight’s helmet again and visit the Church of Iomedae, finding it strangely deserted with all doors locked.  A few polearm strikes and Acid Splash spells later they have utterly destroyed the main church doors, loudly and publicly.  Investigating the inside of the church, they find some healing items & books devoted to the study of Iomedae the Inheritor, but they take nothing and instead opt to pay a carpenter to repair the doors and cover up their crime.  They do this knowing that there were potentially dozens of witnesses…

Before dawn and with their prisoner Riley Kels, they travel 2 miles out of town where Kels claims to have seen the Angel Knight.  Examining the hill themselves, they find no sign of anything and return to town.

As they approach the Longacre Jail in the early hours, Bolgart Caggan, still in the stocks but looking very healthy and healed up from his last tussel with them begins taunting them.  They instantly respond with threats and Bolgart appears to fly into a rage.  He struggles against his restraints as his body morphs and grows in bizarre and grotesque ways.  He shapeshifts into a wereboar!

Bolgart charges Jamal, goring him brutally before being lance-charged down by Freya upon her steed Sleipnir.  They secure him with wrist, leg, and neck shackles and throw him in the jail.  They hire the town carpenter to repair the pillory stocks but instruct him to leave the broken ones out in the open for all to see. They carefully leak information about Bolgart’s apparent curse that he kept hidden for so many years and paint themselves as monster-hunting saviors of the town.

Razelago connects them to Ingoe, the shipmaster at the docks north of town who promises to keep them apprised of all rumors & cargo to come through for only 10gp a week. The party opts out of negotiation and agrees to his price as they are exhausted of haggling, arguing, and fighting with the populace this week.

The normal day to day work of policing a town falls upon the team like a ton of boring bricks they don’t want to talk to.  No new rumors come in. No known criminals are sighted.  Nothing interesting happens, until a report comes in that the Western Gate is open.

The Western Gate of Longacre is a huge stone structure that serves no real purpose as there is no serious connecting wall on either side of it, just a simple fence. An old couple, Bo and Dent Gramel, live there for the past several decades in their retirement from the Chelish Army Artillery Core.  They chit chat with passing merchants every day and maintain the gate, at least they did until Archbaron’s decree fired them and declared the gate closed.

So it’s strange to find it open, and with little else to do, the team investigates. They are quickly and brutally attacked by a tiny fey creature who appears to be a feline humanoid of a type they have never encountered. Draymus, who studies these things, is completely stumped as to what it could be (natural 1). 

After viciously clawing Jamal to such an extent that he passes out from the bleeding, they finally drop the creature with spells from Draymus and Jason.  Bo and Dent watch all of this with great satisfaction, with Bo cheering the “ghost of the gate” onward, until the creature is killed at which point the old couple returns to their home in dismay.  Returning the still-living creature to Razelago, he informs them that it is in fact a fey creature called an Ovinnik. They often attach themselves to a place and are very protective of it.  The creature is turned over to the Archbaron for research purposes.

Moving on from that confusing encounter, they elect to harass the Church of Omedae without fully healing their party from the last encounter. This ends in a very awkward stand-off at the church’s doors with Sword Knight Allamar, her twin sons, and several acolytes of the church.  The party backs off but resolves to renew this conflict at an as-yet undecided but definitely later date.

As the party rests at the Ash House, Jamal watches the church well into the evening.  He sees no one entering or leaving, but after several diligent hours at this post, he sees one of the Longacre deputies that works for them at the jail run past, clearly injured.  Jamal stops him.  

The man has taken a severe beating and is barely on his feet, but he was heading to the Ash  House to inform the team that someone stormed the jail, knocked him out and nearly killed Cimri, and released Riley Kels from jail. Bolgart was left behind, apparently rumors of his curse have spread far and wide (thanks to the team spreading them).

Jamal gathers everyone and they head straight for the Jail. It is as the guard says, Riley has been freed and Cimri nearly killed. She fought the assailants, three humans and one halfling, all masked and fell unconscious from her injuries.  It is also noted that Cimri was bandaged to prevent her death after being defeated.  Freya tracks them for some distance but their footprints disappear both into the streets and into the forest as if they were using Pass Without Trace spells.

The team elects to free Bolgart Caggan for reasons Bolgart does not understand, but he does not question it and walks free into the night.

The next day, the Archbaron is dismayed at these developments. He decides to turn up the heat on the populace, and orders the team to execute whomever they have in the jail.  Since that is now “no one,” they search for someone else to accuse of treason and execute. They quickly decide on Bo Gramel, the old woman who lives by the Western Gate and sassed them when the “ghost” was giving them trouble.

At the Gramel residence there is a brief but violent struggle during which Jason is nearly injured by thrown cookware. The old woman is apprehended.  Construction of a guillotine begins in the church square.  Letting the information sink in to the towns people and also giving themselves time to prepare (for once) they schedule the execution to take place at dusk.

With all available guards, deputies, and party members positioned around the square, the public begins to file in.  Hundreds have come to see if they really will execute old woman Bo for treason.  Cimri and Jamal are hidden within the crowd, Draymus and Jason are on the platform giving a sermon as the crowd gathers close, and Freya is atop Sleipnir, her naginata in hand.

As Jason’s sermon comes to a close and he prepares to pull the lever which will certainly kill the prisoner, rioters draw weapons and storm the stage. At the same time a horse approaches at a gallop. Atop this horse appears to be a woman in a suit of shining silver plate mail with a stylized helmet the crowd has all seen before. The Angel Knight of Longacre has come!

“People of Longacre!” the knight shouts over the sounds of the confused crowd. “Will you let another of your neighbors be murdered by tyrants? The goddess Iomedae and the knights of the Glorious Reclamation are with you! Rise up! Save your friends, your town, and yourselves!” [booktext, PFAP-HVHC]

Jason casts Shield of Faith on himself and pulls the lever, instantly killing old Bo Gramel.  Jamal makes his way towards the Angel Knight, killing a rioter on the way, and manages to slice the Angel across the back. He is immediately suspicious of deception as the strike feels and sounds like it hit leather, not plate armor.  Draymus, through the chaos, casts Cause Fear at the Angel Knight, who instantly begins screaming in terror and trying to regain control of her panicked mount (natural 1).

Freya seizes this opportunity and rides through the crowd, trampling rioters, deputies, and citizens alike as she charges and screams a challenge at the Angel Knight.  As her naginata blade strikes perfectly in the Angel Knight’s chest, the deception is revealed.  Instead of the Angel Knight, the destroyed body of Riley Kels hangs on her blade.  Freya slowly turns, still holding the dead-but-twitching bard aloft in front of Sleipnir who snorts and paws at the ground, anxious to stomp on his master’s enemies.

The rioters are crushed. The crowd flees the terror and chaos of the scene.  The team enjoys a well earned victory as they place both Bo Gramel and Riley Kel’s heads on pikes in the church square.

The scions of law and order in Longacre are relaxing at the town jail after a very public and planned execution of Bo Gramel, and an also very public but less planned execution of Riley Kels, the town’s most popular and talented bard (no, that’s not saying much).

On Freya’s turn to watch, the roving deputy rushes into inform her that the guillotine & severed heads on display have been tampered with.  The coffins stand empty and the remains are missing.  Freya wakes everyone and they investigate the scene.

The only fresh tracks from the coffins lead towards the Church of Iomedae, which stands with it’s doors shut and dim lights shining in the beautiful stained glass windows.

The team gathers all the town deputies they can find (4), and approaches the church around 3 am.  They prepare for a fight in every way they can.  Jason and Draymus pile on the buff spells and they send Jamal to scout the doors.  Jamal occasionally suffers from a lack of focus and this bites into him a little as he’s instantly spotted by the sentry the Iomedaeans have left posted at the front.

Before anyone can react, Jamal has buried two daggers in the chest of the church sentry who stumbles backward through the main doors and sounds the alarm.  All entrances are instantly barred and fortified, so the team turns to regard  those beautiful stained glass windows.

The goddess Iomedae was not always a god. She was once a mortal paladin of Aroden.  Brave and just, she battled evil wherever she found it before finally seeking and passing the test of the star-stone, ascending to godhood.  

Her greatest deeds are now referred to as the 11 Acts of Iomedae. Each of the 11 stained glass windows of the Longacre Church of Iomedae represents one of these sacred acts.  [ http://pathfinder.wikia.com/wiki/Acts_of_Iomedae ]

The largest window in the church is 8′ wide and 6′ tall, depicting Iomedae at the Battle of Three Sorrows in close combat with Erum Hel, Lord of Morghs.  Jason charges the priceless work of art, shattering most panels of it but the iron seams between the glass plates hold.  Looking into the room he sees the shocked faces of four Iomedaean Acolytes, one of which successfully casts Daze on him.

With Freya pushing him through like a battering ram, Jason finally breaches the now destroyed window and the team pours through the opening behind them.  The acolytes put up a brave fight, one even pulling Jamal’s daggers out of his chest and healing himself and his comrades, but they are overwhelmed.

Draymus drops a Grease spell beneath them causing several acolytes to fall as Jason uses the Fire of Asmodeus to ignite the grease.

The team presses the attack as Cimri and Jamal tumble through the enemy lines to flank them.  The acolytes quickly fall and receive Coup De Grace’s for their dedication.  The assault force turns to the double doors leading into the main hall of the church.

Draymus feels an oppressive force against him, as the temple’s consecration immediately goes to work on him for his Dhampir heritage.  Despite this, he manages to cast Enlarge Person on Freya.  Now twelve feet tall and wielding a fifteen foot naginata, the samurai and Jason hammer away at the church doors which have been barred from the other side.

Finally smashing through, they are met with another force of acolytes but also Sword Knight Allamar’s twin sons, Loran and Lacall.  

A bloody battle ensues in this cramped hallway. Draymus and Jason are forced into primarily healing roles as Freya cuts her way through the Iomedaeans but is pierced several times by arrows fired by a strange creature they struggle to keep sight of.  It appears to be a small winged child with a longbow that peeks over the shoulders of the twins only long enough to fire it’s bow, then disappears after.

Jamal and the town deputies are unable to assist in the fight while it rages in that tight hallway, so they exit the church and search for another stained glass window large enough to break through and flank their enemies.  They choose the portrait of Iomedae in Kantaria, a city here in Cheliax to the Northwest that Iomedae once held against constant onslaught for over a year.

The legend goes: Iomedae had been just passing through Kantaria when she detected evil creatures masquerading as the town’s leaders. She struck them down and with the true Lord Mayor away, she took over leadership of the city until his return a year later.  She then gave the Lord Mayor her sword, Heart’s Edge to assist in Kantaria’s defense.  Centuries later, it is the finding of this very weapon by the Hellknights of the Godclaw that touched off the Glorious Reclamation’s rebellion here in Cheliax.

Jamal and the deputies know and consider none of these historical facts as they attempt to push each other through these irreplaceable relics.  Again they find the iron weaving difficult to break through.  Meanwhile the battle inside rages on.

Freya has fought her way into the sanctuary of the church.  She kicks over pews or shatters them with her naginata as she takes the legs out from under the remaining acolytes. While the Iomedaeans continue to heal each other, they are not able to save themselves from Cimri and Jason systematically finishing off any downed foes.

Finally the twins are engaged directly, as Loran moves up to heal an acolyte. Jason lands a brutal hit with his Lucerne Hammer, knocking the young man unconscious and Freya, squeezing into the sanctuary still in her 12′ tall enlarged form, drives the blade of her naginata straight through Loran’s defenseless head.  As the samurai stares down his grief stricken twin brother, she twists the blade, crushing his skull into a bloody pulp.

Still battling the tiny archer, Draymus identifies it as a Kuribu, a lesser angel.  The creature continues to pepper them with arrows, even shooting Cimri as she dashed in to finish off a wounded acolyte.

Finally Jamal and the deputies are able to break through the glass and charge into the sanctuary to aid their allies.  From this angle they can finally see that at the main altar of the church, the Sword Knight Tiileavia Allamar stands casting a spell on the body of Riley Kels laid out before her.  She holds a scroll in her hands and seems frozen in concentration.

Jamal charges the Sword Knight, receiving a vicious shot from Lacall’s heavy mace along the way but the rogue reaches her. Jamal is only able to inflict a minor wound with the edge of his Kukri, but the distraction is enough. She loses her spell of resurrection and Jamal looking over her shoulder sees the corpse of Riley undergo a horrifying transformation.

What first were bands of light pulling closed the massive wound on Riley’s chest and neck now become writhing worms, sewing his flesh together with their bodies, pulling tight to reform him into one structure.  A blue light kindles in his dead eyes.  A long serpentine tongue slides hungrily out of his mouth as he rises and glares at Jamal.  The priestess is horrified and she flees through a curtain towards her study.

Now a heinous undead ghast, the artist formerly known as Riley Kels leaps upon Jamal, paralyzing him with his ghoul-touch and infecting him with ghoul-fever as he claws and clutches the hobgoblin.  

While Riley’s filthy undead tongue writhes over Jamal’s face, Draymus rushes into the room and attempts to command the creature.  A brief battle of wills between the vengeful spirit in the bard’s dead body and the necromancer ensues, and Jamal is released.

Freya and Jason absolutely crush the remaining acolytes and Lacall Allamar. With the other twin’s death the lesser angel has failed it’s mission and departs.  The deputies are unwilling to approach the ghast, even under Draymus’s control.

Sword Knight Allamar returns with her sword and shield, declaring them all abominations and swears she will bring them to Iomedae’s justice.

The Sword Knight proves a formidable foe.  She is enraged at the loss of her sons and the results of her failed resurrection spell and empowered by her temple.  

Flanked by a spiritual sword of Iomedae that flies through the air attacking her enemies and a glowing celestial wolf, she begins channeling positive energy. These waves of invisible light heal her while they burn Draymus and Riley severely.  The necromancer is forced to retreat with his pet undead in tow.

Jamal finally escapes the ghoul paralysis, and leaps into flanking position with Cimri who tumbles past the Sword Knight.  The two dual wielding rogues make quick work of her together, and with her death the summoned guardians disappear.

The Longacre Church of Iomedae is a horrific bloodbath from the front doors all the way through the aisles of pews and up to the altar.  Draymus forces the ghast to submit to destruction by Freya’s naginata and then moves to join Jason at the altar of Iomedae. 

Together they perform the ceremony to unravel the church’s sacred protection spells.  In doing so, they sense a powerful force is trapped inside the stones of the sanctuary and it’s altar.

Their ceremony complete, Jason blesses his lucerne hammer with the power of Asmodeus and shatters the altar.  A shadowy figure appears above the broken stones and immediately lunges in a double sword strike at empty air as if completing a devastating attack frozen in time.  He quickly regains his composure and turns to regard them and the bloodied hall and asks them for the current date.

After learning how long he has been trapped, the man thanks them for saving him and offers them a boon of death or knowledge as payment.  If they choose death, they need only give him a name and that person will die.  If they seek knowledge, those who are worthy can receive the teaching of Archaekek and begin training with him.

The man says he senses several people he has killed are now alive again and this cannot stand, so he must depart immediately.  They briefly consider sending him after one of their enemies, but the people they hate the most in this town are dead on the floor before them. 

They opt for knowledge, and Jamal begins his study as a Red Mantis Assassin. After 24 hours of intense training on many subjects, the man alters his appearance to that of a Cheliax peasant, and hands his sword belt and blades to Jamal.  Bidding them farewell, he walks town the road with a slight limp, headed Southwest. 

Razelago obtains an official decree from Archbaron Fex naming Jason the new head pastor of the Longacre Church, now devoted to Asmodeus instead of Iomedae.  Jason orders all the Iomedaean stained glass windows removed and destroyed. The team personally funds their replacement, spending over 400gp on renovations for the church that begin almost immediately.

But before work can begin, there is the matter of many gallons of blood and guts covering the church floor.  Jason performs a ritual to seal much of the blood into a giant symbol of Asmodeus on the sanctuary floor, then cleans up the rest and covers the symbol with the church pews.

Cimri Staelish is promoted to her aunt’s old job of Sheriff of Longacre.  The team spends some time hanging out at the jail while Jamal begins training as a Red Mantis Assassin.

The team pays a visit to Ingoe, their informant at the docks. He’s heard there is someone with evidence placing Cimri Staelish, the town’s new sheriff, at the scene of Louslik Tannery the night before they announced they were bankrupt.

With the rogue finally completing his training with the Red Mantis Master, they inform him of the plot to smear Cimri’s good name.  Jamal immediately pays the accuser a visit at his home, accidentally kills him (after a brief pause to google: ‘lethal damage below 0′), and spends the next two hours slowly and carefully sneaking his body to the nearby river to dispose of him.

With that danger quelled, they turn their sights on policing the town.  They are not bored for long, as Doctor Rohalendi slips into the church with her face hidden underneath a black hooded cloak and asks to speak with Jason.

She claims to have been approached by some strange people who offered her money if she would only store some things for them for a few hours tonight. Given the amount of money involved and request for secrecy, she knew it was something illegal and thought Jason should be informed.

She does not know where they are getting the items or where they are going next, only that they will arrive sometime after midnight and be picked up before 4 am.  Cimri pieces together the rest of the puzzle and guesses that it is in fact the rebellion and they are likely planning to rob the Longacre Armory.

Longacre is a retirement community largely.  Many former Chelish soldiers chose this sleepy little town to live out their days in peace.  Often they find after returning to civilian life that they no longer need their favorite weapons and gear of war and donate them to the Longacre Armory which is really more of a museum.  

These items are proudly displayed for the public one day a week.  While most of the gear is very worn, some are in fine shape and are expertly crafted, including a small ballista mounted in the main hall and maintained quite well over the years.

Jason and Freya leave their armor behind to enhance their stealth as they discretely approach and enter the armory nearly 11 hours before they believe the heist will begin.  Jamal loads and prepares the ballista to be fired, and connects it to a tripwire attached to the front door of the armory.  Anyone opening that door will have a spear-sized arrow fired at them. 

Freya, Jason, and Draymus take up position in a side room, while Jamal hides in a bush outside the building to keep watch.  Freya and Jason find scale-mail they can wear in the museum and don this to give themselves some protection.  They are already missing their suits of masterwork full plate.  They begin to get nervous around midnight, but as the minutes slowly pass Jason begins to wonder if they might have picked the wrong site.

At 12 minutes after midnight, as they are about to give up the ambush, they hear a key turn the lock at the back of the armory.  The sound of armored feet echoes through the armory.  The team re positions slightly and the footsteps stop as torchlight begins to spill around the corner into the main hall.  Freya draws her longbow and readies a shot.

They hear a voice whisper: “Abort!” And then an answering voice that seems familiar to them responds “No! I want them!”

The voice again floats through the darkness at them: “They’ve messed with the ballista. Don’t touch it!”

With all hopes of surprised dashed, the team attacks. Freya fires her longbow at the first enemy around the corner which turns out to be old Dent Gramel. Dent is wearing a shining breasplate with the symbol of the Chelish Artillery Core across the breast, and wielding a fine longsword and shield.  As he deflects Freya’s first shot, another armored figure leaps out from behind him wielding a bow of her own.

Old Bo Gramel, her head firmly back on her shoulders and looking like the picture of mid-50s human health, growls at them and levels a repeating heavy crossbow at them.  She fires twice in rapid succession, striking Freya once.

Jamal hears the sounds of combat and heads to the front door.  Wisely remembering his ballista trap, he disarms it and charges in the front door to join his team.

Bo and Dent have brought three rangers with them. Fit young folks wearing leather armor, wielding hand axes, and showing the signs of travel and woodland life.  A deadly melee ensues and Freya is nearly defeated by the focused fire of Bo Gramel until Jason and Draymus save her with healing magic.

Jamal tears through the ranger before him and charges Bo, forcing her into defensive posture and leaving poor old Dent to deal with Freya and Jason alone. The man is quickly dispatched along with the remaining rangers.  Draymus repeatedly zaps Bo with a Shocking Grasp spell through his Spectral Hand and she falls unconscious.

The heist is thwarted and the wrongfully (then) accused old woman can now be rightfully branded a traitor along with her husband.

Several events converge at once as we start this session. The fight at Longacre Armory is over, the would-be thieves are thwarted.  El Guapo is riding a rented mule into town after being forced to lay-low away from the Cheliax coast for a while.  As he rides into town and the party processes their prisoners, there is a fire at Longacre Jail.

After politely asking for directions, he walks his mule up to the building and finds smoke pouring out of its windows, and two armed men standing nervously on the porch.  At first they do not notice him as they are looking into the building.

He hears the unmistakable sound of sword play coming from inside the building and he draws his rapier.  The men reach for their swords but before they can unsheathe them, the pirate rushes in and scores a brutal hit on one of them.  They attempt to surround and flank him but El Guapo is too deadly, and his second strike downs the injured man.  The other flees, and El Guapo begins stuffing tobacco into his pipe.

El Guapo suspects that the older woman is deliberately holding back, attempting only non-lethal attacks against her opponent.

As the party is leaving the Longacre Armory, they see the pillar of smoke rising from the neighborhood of the jail.  They leave Draymus in charge of their prisoners, and Jamal, Freya, and Jason rush down the street towards the jail.

Back at the jail, the older woman has prevailed and knocked the younger unconscious.  She picks her up and carries her to the door, finally noticing El Guapo.  He kindly moves aside.

Jason, Jamal, and Freya finally arrive on the scene and instantly identify Rhona Staelish carrying the body of Cimri Staelish out the front of the Jail.  They are confused to see El Guapo standing next to her smoking a pipe on the porch of a burning building.

At the Ash House, Cimri is healed.  The prisoners are interrogated and executed, their heads placed on spikes outside the church.  The party learns from them that the rebellion is storing weapons and supplies at a fort outside of town. 

Fort Estazano has been abandoned for decades, as far as anyone knew.  A small but well fortified structure when it was built, one of it’s two watchtowers has completely collapsed into rubble and from the outside it looks vacant.  The area around the fort is still mostly cleared of trees up to 600′ from the walls, but a tall grass has taken hold in the area.

Sending Jamal in to investigate with El Guapo in tow as backup, the two scouts crawl through the grass to reach the wall of the fort. Jamal uses a potion of Levitate to easily reach the top.  He begins sneaking about, searching for signs of rebellion presence.

No sound that is, until a deep rumbling growl and the flap of huge leathery wings is heard from within the keep.

A monstrous manticore soars out of the center of the fort and perches itself on the wall next to the halfling archer.  The party promptly retreats all the way to town.

Healing and resting in town, they plan their next course of action.  Many interesting ideas are proposed but eventually they settle on going back to the fort prepared for a fight.  Jamal brings with him a potion of Invisibility this time to aid in his stealth.

Freya rushes to his aid and they are able to drive the creature off. Just before it flies out of reach, Jason makes one more mighty throw of his hammer and downs the monster.

Noticing a change in the density of the forest, Jamal halts their progress to study the area.
