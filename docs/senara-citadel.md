# Senara’s Hellknight citadel and the Whisperwood road

The campaign blog’s “Charges Dropped” (P028, following the Black Daggers’ surrender) records a visit to a local Hellknight citadel, Draymus learning a spell, the chapter falling back to protect its fortress, and Freya choosing to hunt unicorns southwest of Senara. Bob drives the party there in the black carriage from Longacre, now drawn by four warhorses.

The source leaves the spell, chapter, fortress name and officer unidentified. The training is a playable story event with a choice of study sequence. It adds a journal entry. The combat spell list stays as it was pending identification. The officer’s design, Bob’s appearance, the building layout, the roadside stop and the conversation are adaptation choices. The first night’s attacks begin in the next section.

Replays 209–214 cover the section from the recovered talisman to the carriage road.
