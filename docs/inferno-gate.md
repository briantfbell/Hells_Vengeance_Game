# Fort Arego’s dungeons and the Inferno Gate

“Down to Earth” (P022) records that the company destroys some denizens of Hell, allies with others, and finds the Inferno Gate on the prison’s lowest level. The gate is a fifteen-foot tear above lava trenches, with falling ash and intermittent black lightning. Visperthul is a heresy devil of Moloch’s Malebolge. After the company exposes Fex’s intent to bind him, Visperthul offers to help close the gate and stay out of Cheliax for a century if they defeat Fex and give him the talisman. They accept.

The two bearded devils, the individual at the stair, and the route through the cellblock are representative adaptation choices. The game treats the Infernal Talisman from Senara (P028) and the Phlegmatic Talisman (P022) as the same object based on the sequence of events. The inventory updates its name when the party learns its purpose. The first pact leaves the talisman in the company’s possession.

Replays 247–253 cover the dungeon entrance, cellblock, parley, gate and first pact.
