# The reception and orphanage

Continue after discovering the empty coffer at Fex’s residence or select **196 · El Guapo’s house party**. Replays **196–202** cover the reception, the orphanage, and the sewer lead.

“Charges Dropped” (P028) has El Guapo host Reclamation soldiers while Draymus and Jason investigate an orphanage formerly tied to the thieves. The account questions whether the party was deliberate intelligence work. It reports that three children saw the investigators and were never seen again. The game preserves that account and leaves their fate unresolved. It uses Black Daggers, the name in P028 and P027. The timeline has a Blood Daggers variant.

The front and side entrances, the specific records found, the reception choices, the fixed D20 faces, and the cast designs are adaptations. Both entrances lead to the same clue from different starting tiles. The return to the residence regroups the five present companions. J’Mal’s next documented appearance is at the guild’s hideout.
