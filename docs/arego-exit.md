# The closing of the Inferno Gate

“Down to Earth” (P022) records that Corrine teleports into Fort Arego with Contessa, an erinyes in the Queen’s service. With a Reclamation battalion already entering the prison, they negotiate with Visperthul. The gate closes and the company may stay in Cheliax if it serves his interests. Corrine and Contessa teleport the party to Egorian. They take rooms at Hotel Barato while Jason reports to the Temple of Asmodeus and Freya to Army Command.

The scene shows the sealing before the party leaves the chamber. The visual sealing and teleport effects, dialogue, Hotel Barato floor plan, and Contessa’s individual appearance are adaptations. The bargain continues to bind the party. This section ends with Jason and Freya away on their reports.

Replays 263–267 cover the intervention, agreement, closure, teleport and hotel.
