# Corrine’s sting and the Crown’s debrief

Replays 305–309 follow P015, “A Vacation from Virtue,” and GM timeline row S096. On 4717.02.24, Corrine coordinates a multi-city sting. Four leading agents are captured or killed and Visperthul’s criminal network in Cheliax collapses. The debrief reports the result without assigning individual outcomes, names, or locations to the four agents.

The sources disagree on Freya’s new rank. S096 names Major, while P015 names Commander. Corrine confirms reinstatement and promotion without using either title. The journal and party menu record Freya’s restored military and Hellknight standing. The debrief does not return Zara Geitta or reactivate Jason’s clerical powers. P015 records Church recognition for Jason here and shows Freya with her plate at a later event. The Queen’s next assignment, conveyed by Corrine, is to settle a feud between two nobles.

The court art and Corrine’s sprite are reused. The location of the debrief, dialogue, and presentation of the reports are adaptations.
