# Reese’s bargain and the death of Fex

“Down to Earth” (P022) records Reese surrendering the Phlegmatic Talisman and pledging his and the company’s fealty to Visperthul in exchange for help. Visperthul heals Reese, summons two bone devils, returns with them through the Inferno Gate, and helps free and heal Jason, Draymus and J’Mal. The company defeats Fex. Afterward they consider turning on Visperthul, but fear the consequences of Reese’s contract.

The game removes the talisman from inventory at signing. The return checkpoint keeps Reese’s restored health and the other characters’ injuries until the rescue. The rematch uses ordinary initiative and support from the two bone devils. The precise healing values, support damage, enemy lineup and El Guapo sitting out the rematch are adaptation choices. The account gives no outcome for Razelago in this specific fight.

Replays 257–262 cover the bargain, summoning, return, rescue, fight and aftermath.
