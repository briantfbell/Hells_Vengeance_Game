# The Barrowood pilgrim trail

P004, “Choices,” describes an overland pilgrimage west of Egorian following Abrogail I’s route. The company carries Sethic in a pack, marks each stone with a drop of his blood, and is ambushed by advanced Glorious Reclamation scouts. A group of paladins allied with a druid nearly stops them. GM timeline row S130 dates the Battle of Barrowood to 1 Gozran, 4717 and records the defeat of the western flank. Row S133 dates the reaffirmation at the final stone to the following day.

The road and grove maps, individual stone locations, enemy numbers, druid’s appearance and tactics, and dialogue are game adaptations. The player marks the first thirteen stones in order. The count saves after every mark. The two fights gate the route at stones three and nine. The party recovers between Vyre and the overland journey. The combat roster follows the five agents named at the covenant. The source does not provide a precise roster for the trail encounters.
