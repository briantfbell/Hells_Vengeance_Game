# Fex’s ambush and Reese’s escape

“Down to Earth” (P022) records Fex and Razelago turning invisible, Jason revealing them with Invisibility Purge, and the party being crippled by Stinking Cloud. Fex dominates El Guapo. Razelago confuses J’Mal. Fex knocks Jason and Draymus unconscious with Chain Lightning. Wounded by bearded devils, Reese takes the Phlegmatic Talisman and follows Visperthul through the Inferno Gate.

The fight uses scripted commands so the campaign outcome is playable. The covering shot, regroup command, wound value, and basalt landing are adaptation choices. If a required actor was downed in the prior dungeon fight, the party gets them back on their feet before the ambush. The escape checkpoint retains the talisman and Reese’s injury. The party’s normal roster resumes outside the solo Malebolge scene.

Replays 254–256 cover Fex’s arrival, the ambush and the landing.
