# Fort Arego’s approach and exterior

“Down to Earth” (P022) places the loose devils’ warning before the attack on Fort Arego. Fex intends to offer the party’s souls to Visperthul, make the Inferno Gate permanent and control the Whisperwood. The party turns against Fex and kills two Reclamation champions before going into the dungeons. The campaign timeline (GM-CHEL-IAX row 83) names Viona Kadarius as a casualty in the assault.

The exact departure route from Senara, the devil’s appearance, the champions’ equipment, the order and placement of the two fights, and the brief recovery on the approach are adaptation choices. The second champion remains unnamed. The exterior ends when the prison door opens. The dungeons begin in the next section.

Replays 240–246 cover the approach, warning, exterior battles and dungeon threshold.
