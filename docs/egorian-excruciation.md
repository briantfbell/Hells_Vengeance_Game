# Oppian’s public excruciation

Continue from the morning at Falco Manor or select **180 · Oppian’s excruciation**. Replays **180–188** cover the charges, the pursuit, the assassination, Freya’s duel, and the resumed ceremony.

“Mid-day Cowboy” (P034) gives the order of events. The campaign timeline and “Charges Dropped” (P028) identify the noble as Duke Fellatio. The game reveals his name when the Dottari respond. The game stages the four bodyguards in the square. El Guapo intercepts one, three charged strikes incapacitate J’Mal, and Freya fights two with the standard combat controls. Draymus arrives with Joe Kek and holds the last guard for El Guapo. Jason stabilizes and heals J’Mal before the Mosswater Tunic carries him away. The scripted escape spends one tunic charge. The Dottari inquiry remains open, and Jaquen’s promised payment is still pending. Freya voices suspicion about the noble’s family as a claim.

The square, heraldry and character art are interpretations. The four opening D20 faces, the Hold Person resistance roll, the healing formula, and the bodyguards’ combat stats are game adaptations. Freya’s armor art includes ready, contact, and recovery poses for katana and naginata.

The browser playthrough started from the prior dawn checkpoint, played the duel with its standard initiative and attack controls, and reloaded both the victory and the completed ceremony. The 640×480 frame stayed fixed and the browser logged no runtime errors. The structural audit checked 91 scenes, 188 replay checkpoints, and 545 frame references. Seeded simulations finished 300 of 300 at full health and 299 of 300 starting at 22 HP with zero potions.
