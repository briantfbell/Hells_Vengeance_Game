- El Guapo leaves Remesiana on another ship, then returns destitute by mule on 21 Neth, 4716 (4716.11.21), after the decrees and before later confrontations. His return is represented at the opening of Political Movement; this is adaptation placement, not evidence that the gate encounter occurred that date. - Fex grants carte blanche. Gathering restrictions, gate controls, and dismissals are the party's policies. Verified limits: six privately, twelve publicly, with family/business exceptions. The later five-person reference has no verified amendment and is not used as a separate law.
- Four notice locations: square notice board, Last Stand, western gate, jail. Jail posting and Rhona's separate dismissal are visibly distinguished. Four departing deputies do not encompass every remaining deputy; retained deputies and replacement guards staff the jail.
- Cimri reports three masked humans and a halfling. Their identities and motive, including the bandaging, remain unresolved. The lost trail suggests possible magic but does not confirm a spell.
- Bolgart's release has no known motive. Fex specifically orders an execution; choosing Bo is the party's retaliation. No invented public charge or speech is added.
