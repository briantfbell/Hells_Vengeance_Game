# El Guapo’s night in Hinji

The vacation narrative P015, “A Vacation from Virtue,” places the tavern visit in Hinji. GM timeline row S110 records the arrest on 12 Pharast, 4717, in Macini. Both sources identify El Guapo’s disguise as Titus Vel and the charge as public drunkenness. The game follows P015 for the location and uses the date from S110. This stop is separate from the earlier Hinji admiralty visit.

The disguise, tavern layout, watch member, dialogue and timing of the watch’s entrance are game adaptations. The player can play along or try to quiet El Guapo. Both choices preserve the recorded arrest. The scene ends with the watch taking him away and leaves the circumstances of his release open.
