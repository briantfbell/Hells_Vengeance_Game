“Mid-day Cowboy” (P034) records the earlier hotel ambush, Svetlana’s help disposing of the bodies, the guard’s disappearance, and Jaquen’s offer. J’Mal suspects Red Mantis involvement. The pair does not identify who killed the neighbor. Jaquen offers the captive guard and 5,000 gp for killing the unnamed noble within two days, plus another 5,000 gp for a public death.
