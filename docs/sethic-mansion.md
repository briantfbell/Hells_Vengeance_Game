# The assault on Sethic’s mansion

GM timeline row S124 places the raid in Egorian on 24 Pharast, 4717. The company captures Sethic and frees Bob Applewood, who is alive and unresponsive. P004, “Choices,” describes the Crown’s sacrifice, the raid and cellar discovery, Freya and Reese bringing Sethic down, Jason stabilizing him, Draymus casting Feeblemind, and the company preparing to carry Sethic in a pack. GM timeline rows S115 and S129 place Bob’s kidnapping on 16 Pharast and his healing in Vyre on 26 Pharast.

The hall and cellar layouts, guard count, fight tactics and dialogue are game adaptations. Freya and Reese take the opening positions and the player controls the full present party during the fight. Sethic’s meter is resolve and emptying it leaves him alive. If Jason or Draymus was knocked out, they recover to 1 HP before stabilizing and restraining Sethic. Bob leaves the cellar unresponsive. El Guapo’s return is reserved for the later sequence where his participation is documented.
