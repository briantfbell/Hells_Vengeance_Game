# The Coffin Recovery in Molthune

GM timeline row S140 dates the rescue to 13 Gozran, 4717 and credits J’Mal with distracting the remorhaz. The campaign blog P001, “Fuck-It List,” describes Reese’s close call scouting the box, Draymus suspecting a Nidalese vampire, and the party using an animated polar-bear carcass as bait. The game has J’Mal direct the diversion and Draymus animate the bear. The remorhaz survives as agreed with Skadi.

Beyond the dragon’s ward, the Wight Mother sends undead through portals. The party drops the coffin. The young woman escapes it and flies clear with the party. She identifies herself as Lady Violetta Pavel, a Nidalese diplomat sent to Queen Abrogail, and produces her credentials. Freya offers her a cup of blood.

The natural 2 during Reese’s scouting, the exact map and enemy positions, and dialogue beyond the recorded events are adaptations. The Violetta portrait is provisional. The Foundry archive identifies her actor as `Actor.moKkAXWInbbCsoPs`. The source portrait returned 404 and the wildcard token reference was unresolved. The game shows her in her ordinary guise.
